Europe, Asia & emerging markets

Asia deploys targeted market support; China margin change starts November 30

China’s exchanges said Sunday that minimum securities-financing margin requirements would fall from 100% to 80% on November 30, Bloomberg reported.[12] Existing contracts can use the revised calculation; abnormal trading can face tighter controls.[12] Korea announced phased stabilization readiness Sunday; Taiwan’s earlier trading curbs are context, not a new Sunday decision.[12]

Context & sources

Why it matters

Interpretation: this increases potential purchasing capacity, not proof of actual inflows or an immediate earnings recovery; distinguish announcement from implementation.[12]

Confidence: High on retrieved observations; interpretation explicitly labelled

  • [12] Bloomberg · Asia support

Europe rejects COP31 draft; no final pact adopted

Al Jazeera’s Sunday update reports that COP31 ended without approval of the proposed agreement after the EU and other parties objected.[13] The draft omitted an explicit mention of fossil fuels, instead referring back to an earlier COP commitment; Ireland’s climate minister said the EU would not endorse that text.[13] Panama also formally objected; the presidency said concerns over developing-country adaptation finance had been addressed, but consensus was absent.[13]

Context & sources

Why it matters

Interpretation: treat this as an unresolved transition-policy and financing signal for Europe and emerging economies, not a new binding emissions rule or quantified commodity-price catalyst.[13]

Confidence: High on retrieved observations; interpretation explicitly labelled

  • [13] Al Jazeera · COP31

OpenAI proposes shared AI development limits

OpenAI chief scientist Jakub Pachocki’s November 22 essay proposes shared AI-development limits, tied to perplexity and other measurements, until understanding and monitoring reach concrete milestones.[16] He acknowledges dataset comparability problems, missing post-training reasoning gains and an unknown loss floor; the disclosed measurements use an internal-codebase snapshot.[16] This is a governance proposal, not a model release or adopted restriction.[16]

Why it matters

Novita discloses MiniMax request-routing privacy issue

Novita says a routing error forwarded some MiniMax requests to MiniMax’s official API instead of Novita infrastructure; it suspended affected models.[17] Requests from May 28–November 11, including OpenRouter traffic, may have been accessed or retained by a third party.[17] A researcher observed embedded-URL/hostname access but no AWS test-key use; automated content review remains under investigation.[17]

Context & sources

Why it matters

Novita recommends revoking or rotating live credentials or secrets included in affected requests; deletion confirmation and clearer provider-retention disclosures remain follow-up checks.[17]

Confidence: Vendor incident disclosure; downstream handling not independently established
