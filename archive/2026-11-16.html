India reviews capital-raising requirements

Empire State: slower growth, stronger hiring

The New York Fed’s November survey index fell eighteen points to 7.8; new orders were 3.0 and shipments 8.8.[22] Employment rose to 18.1, its highest since June 2022, while prices paid fell fourteen points to 45.9 and prices received slipped to 27.2.[22] Capital-spending plans reached 23.0, strongest since December 2022; responses were collected November 2–9.[22]

Warsh: prepare for AI upside and disruption

In Monday’s San Francisco conference remarks, Fed Chairman Kevin Warsh outlined AI scenarios spanning shared productivity gains, higher output with job displacement, and disappointing investment with lower valuations and slower growth.[20] He called them illustrative scenarios, not forecasts, and said Board staff had begun evaluating contingencies with stylized models.[20]

Acquittal tests the boundary between exploit and fraud

Reuters reported that Judge Jessica Clarke acquitted Anton and James Peraire-Bueno Monday in the alleged $25 million Ethereum trading-exploit case.[27] She found insufficient evidence of material misrepresentations supporting wire-fraud and related convictions after an earlier jury deadlocked.[27] Prosecutors alleged a MEV-Boost vulnerability exploit; the defense called the strategy legitimate, and the ruling concerned the charged offenses, not morality or every possible crime.[27]

CPUs, memory & research infrastructure

Vera expands into CPU-centric scientific racks

NVIDIA announced its Vera rack-scale CPU platform Monday.[34] The liquid-cooled design combines 192 CPUs, approximately 300TB of memory and one petabyte of storage; each CPU has 88 cores, 176 threads and SOCAMM2 LPDDR5X memory rated up to 1.2TB/s.[29] Partner racks are expected in early 2027; NVIDIA’s 1.9-times performance and 2.4-times performance-per-watt claims concern one OpenFOAM airflow test, not independently reproduced general results.[29]

Why it matters

AMD adds user access and an HPC assistant

AMD said Monday that Oak Ridge’s Lux supercomputer is open to scientific users, combining EPYC CPUs, Instinct MI355X GPUs, Pensando networking and ROCm in an HPE-built system.[30] Its open-source HPC Assistant, developed with CSC on LUMI and coming to Frontier, targets job submission, profiling, energy visibility and kernel optimization.[30]

AI releases, research & policy

Remote-compute controls follow the ultimate customer

BIS announced an interim final rule addressing remote use of advanced chips through third-country data centers.[31] The displayed text targets substantial compute for entities headquartered or ultimately parented in D:5 countries or Macau, requires ownership diligence, and adds licensing for Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Brazil with conditional exceptions.[32] It schedules November 19 publication and ties effectiveness to publication; Monday’s announcement was not immediate implementation.[32]

Context & sources

Why it matters

Interpretation: cloud operators face customer-and-control diligence beyond physical chip location; verify final text and transition provisions rather than infer a universal cloud ban.[31][32]

Confidence: High on announcement/displayed text; future publication pending

  • [31] BIS · remote-compute announcement
  • [32] Federal Register · remote-compute rule

Nemotron packages chemistry data and adaptable agents

NVIDIA’s Monday Nemotron for Science package combines Nemotron 3.5 Super VL applications, chemistry data and post-training recipes.[35] LitmusBench v0.2 contains nearly 50,000 chemistry-reasoning questions; NVIDIA says the model and dataset are on Hugging Face and the model is optimized for a 128GB DGX Spark.[33] Reported recovery of about 76% of annotated reactions on RxnScribe is a vendor benchmark claim, not independently validated laboratory discovery.[33]

Context & sources

Why it matters

Interpretation: local deployment and domain adaptation make a testable research toolkit; benchmark extraction accuracy is distinct from reproducible tool use or experimentally confirmed findings.[33]

Confidence: High on package announcement; benchmarks not independently replicated
