Macro, central banks, rates & credit

Fed holds rates, with dissent in favor of tightening

The October 28 statement, released at 14:00 EDT (13:00 CDT), maintains the federal-funds target at 3.75%–4.00% by a 10–2 vote.[4] Lisa Cook and Beth Hammack preferred a quarter-point increase.[4] The Committee describes strong activity, resilient domestic spending and elevated inflation, while seeking more information.[4]

Context & sources

Why it matters

Interpretation: the actual decision was a hold, not a cut or a promised future hike.[4] Dissent identifies a policy disagreement, not a guaranteed December outcome. The central bank’s economic characterization is its assessment, not a substitute for independently retrieved activity releases; Thursday GDP is outside this edition.

Confidence: High: primary evidence; interpretation labelled.
