Economy, rates, currencies & commodities

GDP, inflation and layoffs

BEA revised third-quarter real GDP growth from 3.4% to 4.3% annualized; private domestic final sales rose 5.8%.[20] October PCE inflation reached 3.8% year over year, core 3.3%; both rose 0.3% monthly, while real spending edged down less than 0.1%.[19] Initial claims fell 13,000 to 189,000 for the week ending November 21.[25]

Context & sources

Why it matters

Reading: strong summer demand and low layoffs leave little evidence of recession, while October’s inflation-adjusted spending pause cautions against extrapolating the GDP acceleration into year-end.[19][20][25] These are different reference periods, not simultaneous measurements of current growth.[19][20]

Confidence: High · dated evidence

Fed contacts and currencies

The Beige Book reported slight-to-moderate growth in eight districts and no change in four; input costs generally outpaced selling prices.[23] Reuters’ afternoon snapshot showed DXY −0.22% at 103.62, EUR/USD +0.17% at $1.0983, yen +0.06% at 159.13 per dollar, and AUD/USD −0.15% at $0.6628.[26]

Technology, AI, chips & datacenters

SpaceX: conditional authorization

The FCC conditionally authorized backup/emergency radio communications for one million Starmind orbital data-center satellites at 500–1,002 kilometers; primary links would be optical.[31] Milestones run from one satellite by November 25, 2033, to full deployment by 2040—not capacity already built.[31]

Context & sources

Why it matters

Scope: operations must be non-interfering, with semiannual safety reports; satellites above 600 kilometers need a final debris-mitigation modification before deployment.[31] Independent trade reporting describes the economics and engineering as unproven; authorization does not validate commercial viability.[28]

Confidence: High · dated evidence

  • [28] Via Satellite · Starmind
  • [31] FCC · Starmind order

Chip access meets financing

Bloomberg reported lenders pausing or reassessing data-center financing serving ByteDance, Alibaba and Tencent, including added diligence on Bridge Data Centres’ proposed $6.6 billion loan.[29] The report also describes a bank declining a $372 million Digital Halo loan and reassessment of a $480 million Aolani facility—not cancellation of every loan.[29]

Context & sources

Why it matters

Interpretation: compute-access uncertainty can obstruct financing before equipment arrives; the restrictions themselves were announced the previous week, while individual deal accounts rely on sources and limited borrower comment.[29]

Cloudflare: agent retrieval

Cloudflare introduced Search for Agents: site owners can enable automatic AI Search indexing and a discoverable search endpoint via dashboard or API, returning retrieved Markdown rather than a generated answer.[27] Free customers have limits; usage remains subject to AI Search pricing and account allowances.[27]

Microsoft: agentic threats

Microsoft disclosed observations of state-sponsored and financially motivated actors using ARTEX for reconnaissance, credential attacks and web exploitation.[30] The framework dates to July; this is new research disclosure, not a tool launch, and Microsoft says it accelerates familiar techniques rather than inventing attacks.[30]

Context & sources

Why it matters

Scope: Microsoft recommends behavioral detection, fewer exposed services, secrets protection and rapid containment; its observations are not independently replicated prevalence estimates.[30]
