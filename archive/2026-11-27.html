AI, chips & hardware

Memory: a conditional reversal, not an inevitable crash

TrendForce’s original November 27 release sets a base case in which conventional DRAM price increases narrow through 2027, potentially flatten in the second half, and decline in 2028.[40] Its bear case moves the beginning of declines forward to the fourth quarter of 2027, assuming weaker server procurement and broader inventory reductions.[40] The analysis cites power constraints, possible changes to neocloud financing and expanding DRAM bit supply as uncertainties.[40]

Context & sources

Why it matters

Interpretation: the earlier reversal is the downside case, not an observed move or independently validated forecast.[40] Financing and power constraints complicate extrapolating today’s tightness indefinitely.[40]

Confidence: Analyst scenarios · not realized outcomes

AI hardware rivalry reaches court and suppliers

CBS reports Friday counterclaims by OpenAI against Apple in northern California federal court; OpenAI challenges Apple’s treatment of departing employees and their new employer.[41] Reuters, syndicated by the Guardian, reports an additional OpenAI allegation that Apple discouraged hardware suppliers from working on future OpenAI devices.[42] Apple denies suppressing competition and says the counterclaims distract from its own confidential-information allegations.[41][42]

Bloomberg’s report, carried by Mint, says French trade minister Nicolas Forissier supports Dutch concerns over the proposed MATCH Act’s extraterritorial reach and implications for firms such as ASML.[43] He says EU instruments remain available while cautioning against an impulsive response; the proposal is under consideration, not an enacted new export prohibition.[43]
